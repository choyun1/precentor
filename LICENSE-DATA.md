This covers, among others: the chant catalog (`chants/catalog.csv`), the LilyPond encodings of the chants,
the mood vocabulary and the texts' mood tags (`moods.txt`, `texts/moods.csv`), the orders of service
(`services.txt`), the lectionary psalm table (`reference/rcl_psalms.csv`) and the examples.

Not covered, because they are in the public domain or under their own license:

- The psalm and canticle texts are those of the 1979 Book of Common Prayer of the Episcopal Church, which is in the public domain.
- The chants are in the public domain: each was published before 1931, so it is in the public domain in the United States, and its composer died more than 70 years ago where that is known. The chants marked as from the *New Cathedral Psalter Chants* (1909) were transcribed from the scan at archive.org.
- The psalm tone formulas (`reference/psalm_tones.json`) come from the jgabc project, released into the public domain (the Unlicense).
- The EB Garamond fonts in `style/fonts/` are under the SIL Open Font License 1.1 (`style/fonts/OFL.txt`).
